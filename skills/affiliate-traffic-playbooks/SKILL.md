# Affiliate traffic playbooks

Distilled from EP's (@eptwts) knowledge base, eptwts.com. These are EP's opinionated operator plays, attributed to him rather than presented as universal facts. This skill teaches the whitehat, durable versions; patched and unethical plays are listed only so you recognize them and extract the transferable lesson.

Each play below is a repeatable checklist for turning attention into affiliate commissions. They share one spine: pick a proven offer, borrow a distribution pattern that already works, produce at volume, and store unconverted traffic in an owned channel (email or Telegram) so it's never lost.

## When to use

- User has picked an offer (see `affiliate-and-clipping-onramp`) and needs the exact operating procedure.
- User wants to build a clipper/UGC team on performance pay.
- User wants a low-risk beginner deal with an offer owner.
- User wants to repurpose a creator's YouTube knowledge into an X account or lead magnets.
- User wants cheap, delegable content formats (slideshows, translated videos) for volume.
- User wants to mine promotion angles from competitors' videos with AI.
- User asks about mass-upload bots, content lockers, giveaways, or buying engagement.

## Core principles

**Borrow distribution that already works.** Every play starts by finding whops/creators already converting and reverse-engineering their traffic - it almost always comes from 1-2 channels (YouTube, paid ads, or X). Don't invent; replicate the pattern with your link.

**Insert an owned channel into every funnel.** EP calls this the crucial step: email list or Telegram between the content and the affiliate link, so unconverted traffic is stored instead of lost. The owned channel later becomes the launch asset for your own product in the same niche.

**Lead magnets are the unit of work.** Batch-create them, post them, pitch the affiliate link at the end of each. The cadence in the mature system is one lead magnet a day.

**Unbranded beats branded on X.** EP: branded accounts flop on X; create an unbranded niche account.

**Delegate production, own the system.** A VA builds the clip catalog; performance-paid clippers edit; cheap labor produces slideshows. Your job is offer selection, funnel design, and the list.

**Pair huge traffic with a strong offer.** The transferable lesson from EP's dead mass-upload play: raw traffic can be manufactured, but pointing it at a trash CPI offer was his big mistake. Check payout per visitor first.

**Platform-dependent arbitrage dies without warning.** Extract profits fast and diversify. Anything that exploits an upload-limit or duplicate-content quirk is on a timer.

**Recognize the manipulation patterns as a consumer; don't deploy them.** EP documents how self-help "cults" are built (see Checklists). Use it to read the market, not to run on people.

## Workflow

1. **Confirm the offer is vetted.** Run the scorecard in `affiliate-and-clipping-onramp`. If the offer isn't proven (EPC, conversion rate, affiliates earning), stop and pick another before running any play.

2. **Pick the play by situation.**
   - Beginner, no audience, no capital: Play 2 (content-boost deal) or Play 3 (broke-start replication).
   - Some budget or ability to recruit: Play 1 (clipping-and-affiliate at scale).
   - Comfortable with AI tooling and want an X presence: Play 4 (YouTube-to-X engine).
   - Want cheap delegable volume: Play 5 (language arbitrage - with rights) or Play 6 (AI slideshows).
   - Need angles before any of the above: Play 7 (AI angle-mining) first.

3. **Design the funnel before producing content.** Content -> lead magnet -> owned channel (email/Telegram) -> affiliate link inside the channel and inside each magnet. Write the DM close script if you'll close in DMs.

4. **Run the chosen checklist below.** Print it. Tick boxes.

5. **Measure payout per 1,000 visits.** If low, change the offer or lander before scaling volume.

6. **Scale or stack.** Once one play produces, either scale it (more clippers, more accounts, more magnets) or stack a second play on the same list. Once the list is sizable, approach top-earning offer owners for higher commissions (EP: many give 50%).

## Checklists / templates

### Play 1: Clipping-and-affiliate at scale (EP, 2024-12-18)

- [ ] Pick a high-earning niche (trading, crypto, dropshipping)
- [ ] Spreadsheet all top creators in that niche
- [ ] Build a catalog of 1,000+ clips from their content (or have a VA do it)
- [ ] Recruit a team of 10+ US-located clippers on performance-based pay (find them in "internet money" Discords)
- [ ] Send them the clips with editing instructions
- [ ] Set up a newsletter with a free lead magnet (e.g. an e-book); funnel viewers into the list
- [ ] Once the list is sizable, approach owners of top-earning Whop communities for affiliate deals (many give 50%)

Note: confirm you have permission/licensing to clip the creators' content; many run official clipping programs.

### Play 2: Beginner deal - content boost for promotion (EP, 2025-02-26)

- [ ] Go to Whop's affiliate-offers page; filter to programs paying 50%+ commission
- [ ] Pick an appealing program; find the owner's socials
- [ ] DM a proposal (casual, peer-like): you run an X page promoting their product; they engage with your tweets to boost them in the algorithm
- [ ] Once agreed, batch-create 10 lead magnets
- [ ] Post them; pitch your affiliate link at the end of each

### Play 3: Broke-start replication with an owned channel (EP, 2025-09-25)

- [ ] Browse Whop discover by keyword to surface top-performing whops in high-value niches (trading, sports betting, bizopp)
- [ ] Confirm good affiliate commissions
- [ ] Find their socials; reverse-engineer the organic strategy that's working
- [ ] Create accounts replicating that content style, promoting the whop via your referral link
- [ ] Crucial: insert an owned alternate channel (email list or Telegram) so unconverted traffic is stored
- [ ] Promote the affiliate link inside that channel alongside related valuable content
- [ ] Later: use the owned channel as the launch asset for your own product in the same niche

### Play 4: YouTube-to-X transcript engine (EP, 2025-12-02; "done right this clears $10k/month")

- [ ] Find whop creators selling specific knowledge (trading, marketing, business)
- [ ] Reverse-engineer their distribution (usually 1-2 channels: YouTube, paid ads, or X)
- [ ] Isolate whops driving traffic from YouTube that are NOT posting on X
- [ ] Extract all their video transcripts; have Gemini distill key teachings into a bullet list
- [ ] In Claude, use the skill-builder to turn that list into a "tweet idea generator" skill
- [ ] Create an unbranded X account for the niche (branded accounts flop)
- [ ] Use the skill to generate lead-magnet ideas; run them as "reply for X" automations
- [ ] Initial boost - whitehat version: get the offer owner and any partners to engage with your first tweets (Play 2 deal), and engage manually in the niche for the first weeks. (EP's original step here was paying an account for engagement; see dead/don't-run section.)
- [ ] Ongoing: one lead magnet a day, affiliate link in each, close in DMs, funnel everyone to email or Telegram

### Play 5: Language-arbitrage promo videos (EP, 2025-02-07)

- [ ] Find a YouTube video that successfully promotes an info product
- [ ] Secure rights: get the creator's/owner's permission to translate and re-publish, or produce your own video on the same angle (re-uploading someone else's video without permission is copyright infringement - this caveat is an addition, not from EP)
- [ ] Download it (EP used cobalt.tools)
- [ ] Get the transcript (EP used youtubetotranscript)
- [ ] Translate to Spanish (or target language) with DeepL
- [ ] Generate a voiceover with ElevenLabs
- [ ] Overlay and sync the voiceover on the video
- [ ] Get an affiliate link for the same product; put it in the description

### Play 6: AI slideshows on TikTok (EP, 2025-06-03)

- [ ] Use ChatGPT to generate an emotional, engaging storyline that indirectly promotes your product
- [ ] Have it split the story into multiple slides
- [ ] Generate background images (EP used gpt-image-1)
- [ ] Delegate production to cheap labor
- [ ] Post at high volume daily
- [ ] Lane check: this is the "mass-produced slop for cheap traffic" lane - run it unbranded, not under your personal brand (see `brand-voice-and-authentic-ai-writing`)

### Play 7: AI angle-mining for promotion ideas (EP, 2025-04-13)

- [ ] Use Perplexity (or any transcript tool) to download transcripts of videos that appear to promote products like yours
- [ ] Define a JSON profile template of what to extract (hook, problem framed, mechanism claimed, proof shown, CTA, objections handled)
- [ ] Send template + transcripts to Gemini; let the AI filter which videos actually promote a similar product - no manual verification needed
- [ ] Output: a bank of long-form promotion angles; feed into Play 4 or your VSL scripts

### Recognize these patterns as a consumer; don't deploy them (EP's field notes, Feb 2025)

- **The 90/10 cult formula:** 90% generic-but-beneficial advice (train, eat clean, study, network) buys uncritical acceptance of 10% indoctrination (feel superior, everything is wrong with the world, you'll surpass everyone). Because the 90% visibly helps, the 10% is never questioned.
- **Odd-behavior asks are compliance tests:** extreme diets, cutting off friends, rituals - same mechanism as gang initiations. If followers comply with no clear benefit, the leader has proof of how far they can be pushed.
- **The self-help manipulation sequence:** identify deepest insecurities -> show a "past self" with the same problems and a dramatic transformation -> make viewers feel broken -> position yourself as the one-size-fits-all solution -> storytelling for relatability -> frame common problems as catastrophic for urgency -> repeatedly display results -> brush off criticism, build an echo chamber -> us-vs-them.

Use this list to audit gurus and offers you're considering promoting. An offer built on this sequence is a reputational liability for your list.

## Anti-patterns

- Running any play on an unvetted offer.
- Skipping the owned channel. Traffic that goes straight to an affiliate link is lost.
- Branded X accounts for niche affiliate content.
- Manual verification of every competitor video - let the AI filter (Play 7).
- Buying engagement, upvotes, or followers to "kickstart" an account. EP describes paying for X engagement; it's ToS-violating - don't run it.
- Re-uploading other people's videos (translated or clipped) without rights.
- Fake giveaways or content lockers that never pay out. EP ran one at 16 and labels it ethically questionable and patched.
- Duplicate mass-uploading through APIs. Dead, and a ban.
- Deploying the cult/manipulation sequence on your audience.
- Under your personal brand, shipping the slop lane. Keep mass-volume AI formats unbranded.
