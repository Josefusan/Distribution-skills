# Monetization Playbooks

Distilled from EP's (@eptwts) knowledge base, eptwts.com. These are EP's opinionated operator heuristics, not universal facts.

This skill helps choose a niche by the value of the viewer and then execute one of EP's full playbooks as a checklist. Core thesis: you need the right views, not many views. Niche profits beat virality; serve markets where people already spend freely; match platform to audience sophistication; and distribution is only one piece - what you sell and how you sell it separates five figures from seven through the same channel.

## When to use

- The user is choosing a niche or business model to start.
- The user has reach (followers, views, a page network) but little revenue.
- The user is a beginner with no product asking for a concrete plan.
- The user asks whether faceless/operator models work.
- The user wants to compare X vs YouTube vs Instagram vs TikTok for making money, not for growth.
- The user asks about affiliate, UGC, local events, Telegram channels, or CPA as an entry point.

## Core principles

**You need the right views, not many views.** EP: a video with 10M views can earn $0 while 1k views of business owners with an expensive problem earn $6,000. A ragebait fitness account with 1M+ views per video made nothing; his faceless informational channel averaging 5,000 views did five-figure months in software sales. Pick the niche for the value of the viewer.

**Niche profits beat virality.** An 8M-subscriber YouTuber with a full team made $50k/month while 5k-subscriber channels pull $100k/month at 95% margins (2025-12). A 4,000-subscriber trading channel made EP $100k-$150k lifetime; a 5k-subscriber fitness YouTuber averaging 1k views runs a $60k/month coaching program because every video is proof-plus-process.

**Trading is by far the best niche to sell info in - serve markets where people already spend freely** (trading, gambling, sports betting, bizopp). Most of EP's money over five years came from trading without ever trading: sell to the market, don't participate in it. Where money flows you can also facilitate deals and take a cut.

**Match platform to audience sophistication.** Short-form and YouTube audiences are less sophisticated and easier to sell info to; Instagram is wantrepreneur consumers; X is sophisticated operators and the top platform for high-quality leads. On X you sell to the gurus themselves - weak offers are seen through instantly, genuine ones make serious money ($50k of high-ticket closed in two weeks off a single Typeform).

**X monetization requires real expertise.** EP made multiple six figures in his first year of posting; his first launch did $37.5k at 99% profit in one day ~30 days after his first tweet, by giving away nearly everything free so people who got value paid for structure. If you're remotely successful in any vertical, X can roughly double your income; without expertise it's a terrible place to get rich.

**100k+ followers and only $10k MRR is a strategy problem, not a reach problem.** Design content backward from who buys. Flex/lifestyle marketing attracts the "vulnerable class"; value-plus-trust with direct checkout attracts businesspeople. The quiet $300k+/month operators share two traits: authority built on a genuine specialty, and videos that function as VSLs for their products.

**Distribution is only one piece.** What you sell and how you sell it can be the difference between five and seven figures through the exact same channel.

**A brand also monetizes as pure connection.** Matching people who need something with people who provide it, with no fulfilment - deal flow arrives in DMs and referrals become a revenue stream.

**Making $1 online is a transferable proof-of-concept.** A $27 lead-scraping Chrome extension promoted with four UGC TikToks a day (~1k views each) made ~$54/day passively; a system that earns while you sleep can be scaled 100x. Beginners wrongly dismiss it as too small.

**If you can sell info products you can sell anything.** Convincing cold audiences to pay for intangible value in a hyper-competitive market builds sales muscle nothing else forces.

## Workflow

Gather: skills/knowledge the user actually has, capital, time, willingness to show face, existing assets (pages, lists, audience), and target income.

1. **Score the niche by viewer value, not view potential.** Ask: does money already flow here (trading, betting, bizopp, B2B with expensive problems)? Is there a painful insecurity with real knowledge behind it (fitness, dating, skincare, finance)? Prefer the niche the user knows over the lucrative one they don't.

2. **Match the platform to the sophistication of who buys.** Consumer info/mentorship → short-form, YouTube. Wantrepreneurs → Instagram. Operators/B2B/high-ticket → X (only with real expertise).

3. **Pick the playbook by starting position** (decision table below), then run its checklist.
   - No skills, no money, no audience → Level-0 baseline, Beginner affiliate, or Broke-beginner UGC retainer.
   - Real knowledge in an insecurity niche → $0-to-$10k insecurity playbook or Faceless niche content formula.
   - Marketer who doesn't want to be the face → Faceless trading-brand model, Multiply through operators, Influencer piggyback (takedown risk).
   - Has traffic but weak revenue → Complete minimal online business (fix the offer and funnel).
   - Wants a page-network business → Telegram crypto channel, College community pages, Local events.
   - Wants to learn direct-response cheaply → CPA as training ground, then graduate.

4. **Run the checklist.** Set a first-dollar deadline: EP's rule is a sale within the first few thousand views or change approach.

5. **If reach exists but revenue doesn't**, run the "100k/$10k" diagnostic: who does the content attract (vulnerable class vs buyers)? Does each video function as a VSL for a product? Is there a direct checkout? Fix strategy before buying more reach.

6. **Scale by multiplication, not more content.** Once a model works, add operators/creators under revshare with ownership secured up front, or monetize as pure connection (deal flow, referrals).

## Checklists / templates

### The complete minimal online business (EP, 2025-03)
1. [ ] Find a validated product or service, replicate it, make it better.
2. [ ] Landing page (bolt.new) with a prominent "buy now" button; payments through Whop.
3. [ ] Find a content style in your niche that demonstrably gets views; double down.
4. [ ] Email list with an automated sequence that sells for you.
5. [ ] Move content traffic to the list with a genuinely high-quality free lead magnet.
6. [ ] Daily value emails; pitch the product once a week.

### $0-to-$10k/month insecurity playbook (EP, 2025-08)
1. [ ] Pick a profitable insecurity you have real knowledge in (obesity, lack of athleticism, dating, skincare).
2. [ ] Gamified info product promising a clear transformation in a set timeframe ("3-phase superhuman athleticism program," "90-day fat-to-shredded sprint"); price $15-$50 - cold-traffic sales are emotional impulse purchases.
3. [ ] Sales page tapping the emotional pain, highlighting the transformation via a "unique mechanism," never the hard work.
4. [ ] Research organic formats that convert - browse Reels for entertaining-plus-educational trending formats (study Peter Peptides-style videos).
5. [ ] Post ~10 videos/day soft-shilling the guide; monitor engagement and conversion; adapt. Edge = execution volume + metric-driven refinement.

### The "level 0" baseline business (EP, 2025-09)
1. [ ] Faceless TikTok page around a common painful insecurity (finance, dating, appearance, health).
2. [ ] Email list; send genuinely useful advice.
3. [ ] Offer that resolves the insecurity (course, PDF, done-with-you).
4. [ ] Direct-response landing page.
5. [ ] Launch to the list; monitor conversions; adjust pricing and copy; iterate. Don't attempt any other model until this works.

### Faceless niche-audience content formula
- [ ] Point out a pain the audience has.
- [ ] Deliver value for 90% of the video.
- [ ] Pitch your info product as the shortcut in the last 10% (e.g., discipline channel → "90-day self-improvement challenge").

### Influencer piggyback method (takedown risk)
1. [ ] Find an influencer with monetizable expertise (Huberman, Knees Over Toes Guy, Paul Saladino).
2. [ ] Create the info product their audience wants (e.g., a testosterone protocol for Huberman viewers).
3. [ ] Repurpose and mass-post their clips to TikTok, Reels, Shorts.
4. [ ] ~3-second CTA to your product at the end. EP: quick $10k at ~$7 per 1,000 views; main risk is takedowns. Prefer licensed/original clips or a clipping-program arrangement to make this durable.

### Faceless trading-brand model
1. [ ] Hire a profitable trader as the face.
2. [ ] Build infrastructure and team (script-writers, editors, thumbnail designers).
3. [ ] Split profits 50/50 (EP ran one to 6 figures in a year fully faceless).
4. [ ] Evolved version: own a small revshare in a few creators for setting up their offers and operating growth (~$15k/month for six months, practically no ongoing work, 2026-03).

### Multiply yourself through operators
- [ ] Once genuinely good at something, invest in others' development in that skill; take a percentage.
- [ ] Get ownership or revshare up front; structure mutual dependence, or you'll be cut out.

### Telegram crypto-channel ad-revenue playbook
1. [ ] Create a crypto Telegram group.
2. [ ] Repost memecoin calls from public accounts (optionally automated across 10 channels with a repost bot).
3. [ ] Drive traffic via TikTok slides or a crypto YouTube channel.
4. [ ] At 1,000 members Telegram monetization unlocks; projects buy ads.
5. [ ] Cross-promote across your channel network. Benchmark: one 100k+ channel made $400/day from ads; private paid calls channel sellable on top.

### College community pages
1. [ ] Instagram page per major American college.
2. [ ] Hire a broke student on each campus to run it.
3. [ ] Grow via giveaways requiring a story repost to enter.
4. [ ] Charge local businesses and event promoters for promotion.

### Broke-beginner UGC retainer play (EP, 2025-03)
1. [ ] Produce promo videos for SaaS and consumer apps to build reps on camera and in editing.
2. [ ] Build a public catalog and ideally a following.
3. [ ] Pitch founders on being the face of their brand for a monthly retainer with unlimited content. Wedge: founders hate camera; per-video UGC is expensive; the catalog is proof.
4. [ ] 2026 adjacent: spawn new distribution channels for funded startups (YouTube, X, IG, LinkedIn) - huge growth budgets reward demonstrable attention.

### Beginner affiliate path
- [ ] Affiliate for a proven digital product instead of creating your own: social pages in its niche → collect leads → refer.
- [ ] "Sell info without info": ask established Whop community owners to affiliate - most give a 50% revshare link and teach traffic.

### Local events business
- [ ] Build local Instagram/TikTok pages that own a city's nightlife audience.
- [ ] Monetize the events; let reputation in those communities compound as the moat.

### CPA/sweepstakes as training ground
- [ ] Understand it as education only: tiny conversions (~$8; email submits ~$2), constant ToS fights. Graduate to your own products.

## Anti-patterns

- Chasing views in entertainment niches with no expensive problem behind them.
- Building for virality with a team when a 5k-subscriber proof-plus-process channel out-earns it.
- Selling "air" on X to a sophisticated crowd, then concluding "Twitter doesn't convert."
- Flex/lifestyle marketing (attracts the vulnerable class; reads as scam signaling).
- Dismissing a $54/day system as too small instead of scaling it.
- Trading the market yourself when the money is in selling to it.
- Giving operators/creators your systems without ownership or revshare secured first.
- Mass-posting an influencer's clips without a plan for takedowns.
- EP describes a Reddit/Quora play using switched accounts and paid upvote services, a "paywall-and-leak" PDF seeded on leak forums, and a Robux CPA offer-wall targeting children. These are ToS-violating / manipulative / patched - they are in the references for completeness only. Don't run them.
