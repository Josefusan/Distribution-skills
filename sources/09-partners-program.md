# EP's Personal Brand Partnership Program (Application)

Source: https://partners.eptwts.com — scraped 2026-09-15. Useful as a template for vetting operators for a publishing/DWY (done-with-you) personal-brand model.

> I've spent the past year taking people who are great at what they do (developers, marketers, founders) and turning them into public figures on X & YouTube with a proven, repeatable framework.
> If you think you're a good fit & want to work with me, fill this out honestly - I read every application. (p.s. I don't charge for this)

## Application questions (verbatim)

1. What do you go by?
2. Email
3. Telegram username (must have, Telegram is the primary comms platform for this program)
4. What do you do?
5. What's your specialized knowledge? What are you genuinely great at?
6. What have you achieved with what you do? What are you most proud of?
7. How much $ does it currently net you? — A. Under $2k/month · B. $2k-$5k/month · C. $5k-$10k/month · D. $10k-$25k/month · E. $25k-$50k/month · F. $50k-$100k/month · G. $100k+/month · H. Prefer not to say
8. Is that income from a job or your own business? — A. A job (employed) · B. My own business / freelancing · C. A mix of both
9. Do you have any products or services you sell? — A. Yes · B. No, not yet
10. If yes - what do you sell?
11. Are you comfortable getting on camera? — A. Yes, fully comfortable · B. Somewhat - willing to get there · C. No
12. Can you show your face / presence in your content? — A. Yes · B. No
13. Would you prefer to stay anonymous? — A. Yes, I want to stay anonymous · B. No, happy to be public · C. Flexible
14. Can you record videos with your voice? — A. Yes · B. No
15. How would you rate your writing skills? — A. Strong - I write a lot and enjoy it · B. Decent - I can get my ideas across · C. Weak - writing doesn't come naturally to me yet
16. What's your experience with social media?
17. Have you ever made content before? — A. Yes, I create regularly · B. Yes, I've dabbled · C. No, never
18. If yes - what kind of content, and how did it go?
19. How comfortable are you with creating content overall? — A. Very comfortable · B. Somewhat comfortable · C. Not yet, but willing to learn
20. How much free time can you commit to this per week? — A. Under 5 hours · B. 5-10 hours · C. 10-20 hours · D. 20+ hours
21. Can you write 2-3 tweets per day? — A. Yes · B. No · C. Not sure yet
22. Can you record at least one YouTube video per week? — A. Yes · B. No · C. Not sure yet
23. What do you want your brand to do for you? What's the goal outcome?
24. What opportunities should it bring?
25. What do you want to sell?
26. Give a few examples of topics you could write or create content about
27. Links to your work (X, YouTube, portfolio, GitHub - anything that shows what you do)

## What the form reveals about the vetting model

- Filters for real specialized knowledge and achievements first (anti-larper).
- Segments by income and whether it's a business (operators over employees).
- Checks camera/face/voice/anonymity preferences to route toward faceless vs. on-camera paths.
- Requires a minimum output commitment: 2-3 tweets/day + 1 YouTube video/week.
- Asks what they want to sell — the brand exists to feed an offer.
